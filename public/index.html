ABOUT US

The messages that tell cells which proteins to make are encoded in messenger RNA, or mRNA. The process of mRNA production and degradation is usually tightly regulated.

However, in some childhood cancers, this regulation is lost. In particular, in DICER1-related cancers, a key component of this regulation mechanism, known as microRNAs, is lost. We study how dysregulation of mRNA production and degradation leads to childhood cancer, and seek ways to exploit this dysregulation as a therapeutic vulnerability.
