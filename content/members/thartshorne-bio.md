Research Background
-------------------

My research experience began at New Mexico State University while taking an independent research lab course for my Bachelor’s degree. Once I saw how the information that I had learned in my genetics classes could be applied in the lab to solve real-life problems, I was hooked. I am excited to further my research experience by studying pediatric cancer in the Chen lab prior to applying to medical school, for the specialty is a great way to merge my academic interest in molecular genetics and personal passion for working with children.


When I'm not in the lab
-----------------------

Most of the time I can be found exploring new parts of the city looking for the best coffee shops and restaurants, or doing any outdoor activity -- hiking, biking, camping, etc. I also love to cook and bake from scratch, and I try new recipes often!
