Welcome to our [newest member](/members)!

We're excited to welcome Peter Schoettler, a UT Southwestern
[Pediatric Hematology-Oncology fellow](https://www.utsouthwestern.edu/education/medical-school/departments/pediatrics/divisions/hematology-oncology/fellow-education/current-fellows.html). He brings with him a long track record of
working with the [*DICER1* syndrome](https://www.ppbregistry.org/) community
and will work on characterizing the biology and treatment of *DICER1*
syndrome tumors.
